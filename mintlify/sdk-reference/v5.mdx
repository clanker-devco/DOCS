Clanker v5 is live on [clanker.world](https://www.clanker.world/deploy) and through the [deploy API](/general/token-deployments/v5-deployments). v5 support in clanker-sdk is coming soon.

Until then, the [v4 Clanker Class](/sdk-reference/v4) covers v4 tokens, and v5 tokens are managed from the token's [Admin page](/general/managing-a-token) on clanker.world.
