Clanker v3.1 deploys tokens into Uniswap v3 pools with single-sided liquidity, an optional creator buy and an optional time-locked vault.

## Core contracts

Clanker v3.1 consists of five main contracts:

1. [**ClankerToken**](/references/core-contracts/clankertoken-v3.1.0-and-v4.0.0): A Superchain-compatible ERC20 contract.
2. [**Clanker**](/references/core-contracts/v3.1/clanker-direct-contract-deployments): The primary contract. It handles token deployment, Uniswap v3 pool creation and single-sided liquidity position placement. Users can optionally make an initial swap and deposit a portion of the token supply into a time-locked vault.
3. **ClankerDeployer**: A helper library that reduces the code size of the Clanker contract.
4. [**ClankerVault**](/references/core-contracts/v3.1/clankervault): Lets creators manage and collect their locked tokens.
5. [**LpLockerv2**](/references/core-contracts/v3.1/lplockerv2): Lets creators manage and collect their portion of the trading fees generated by their token's pool. The Clanker team also uses it to collect its portion of the trading fees.
