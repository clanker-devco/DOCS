---
title: "Compatible Trading Platforms"
---

Trading platforms, aggregators and wallets known to support Clanker pools are listed below for each version, starting with Clanker v5. If you run a trading platform or venue and want to integrate with Clanker's pools, reach out through [Contact](/references/contact).

## Clanker v5

Clanker v5 tokens trade in Uniswap v4 pools that use their chain's Clanker v5 hook. Every trade pays the token's buy or sell tax. Clanker v5 launches are open on chain on Base and Elysium. The platform list below applies to Base.

Known compatible trading platforms as of November 2026:

### Trading platforms and frontends

* clanker.world
* GMGN
* Uniswap

### Wallets

* Rainbow
* Rabby
* Coinbase Wallet

## Clanker v4

Clanker v4 deploys tokens into Uniswap v4 pools with custom Uniswap v4 hooks. Because of these custom hooks, not every trading platform or venue supports trading through Clanker v4 pools. Many Telegram trading bots on Base don't support Uniswap v4 pools or Clanker's Uniswap v4 hooks.

Known compatible trading platforms as of July 8, 2025:

### Trading platforms and frontends

* clanker.world
* Uniswap
* Oku Trade
* LlamaSwap
* Based Trading Bot

### Aggregators

* 0x / Matcha

### Wallets

* Rainbow
* Rabby
* Coinbase Wallet

## Clanker v3.1 and earlier

Clanker v3.1 and earlier versions deploy tokens into Uniswap v3 pools, which almost all trading venues support.

You may run into issues trading a Clanker token paired with a token other than WETH if the paired token has no liquid paired token / WETH pool. In that case, hold the paired token directly, so the trade doesn't try to hop pools (ETH to the paired token to the Clanker token).
