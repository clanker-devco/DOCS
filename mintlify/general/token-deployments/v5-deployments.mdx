<Tip>
**Live**: Clanker v5 deployments are live on Base at [clanker.world/deploy](https://www.clanker.world/deploy) and through the deploy API.

</Tip>

A Clanker v5 token has a 1 billion supply in a Uniswap v4 pool paired with ETH or USDC, and charges a buy and sell tax of 1% to 10% instead of an LP fee. See [Token Deployments](/general/token-deployments) for the full overview, and [Creator Rewards and Fees](/general/creator-rewards-and-fees) for how the tax is split.

<Info>
`GET /api/metadata/factories` returns the live v5 launch policy: tax bounds, Clanker fee, sniper fee and dividend minimum.

</Info>

See [v5](/references/core-contracts/v5) in Core Contracts for what each contract does, and [Deployed Contracts](/references/deployed-contracts) for the addresses.

### Deploying on clanker.world

On [clanker.world/deploy](https://www.clanker.world/deploy), choose Base and set **Token type** to **V5**. Then set the buy and sell tax and, optionally, holder dividends, burn and the sniper fee. See [Clanker.world Deployments](/general/token-deployments/clanker.world-deployments) for each field.

### Deploying with the API

Use `POST /api/tokens/deploy` with `protocol: "v5"` and `tax: { buyFee, sellFee }`. The other token fields and partner authentication are the same as for v4 deployments. See [Deployment](/api-reference/authenticated/deployment).

* `buyFee` and `sellFee` give the total trade tax in percent, including the Clanker fee.
* Optional tax fields: `dividends`, `burn`, `minimumDividendHolding`, `antiSnipeEnabled`, `integrator` and `metadataEditable`.
* `dividends`, `burn` and the integrator `share` are percentages of the tax after the Clanker fee. Together they must not exceed 100.
* `minimumDividendHolding` is a whole-token decimal string.
* `integrator: { address, share }` gives an integrator part of the tax. The default share is 0.
* `metadataEditable` (default `true`) lets the creator change the image and metadata after launch, and verify the token.
* `pool.pairedToken` can be WETH (for an ETH pair) or USDC. v5 does not accept v4 fees, other pool settings, vaults, airdrops or pool extensions.
* A Droid launch (`agent` with `legionFundingRecipient`) needs the USDC pair.
* Use `dryRun: true` to simulate a deployment.

The `/api/tokens/deploy/v4` route accepts v4 deployments only.

### Rewards and Claims

v5 creator rewards collect in the FeeEscrow, which holds one balance per recipient and currency for all of that recipient's v5 tokens. A claim pays the whole balance. Creators claim on the token's Admin page on [clanker.world](https://www.clanker.world/): `https://www.clanker.world/clanker/TOKEN_CONTRACT_ADDRESS_HERE/admin`. See [Creator Rewards and Fees](/general/creator-rewards-and-fees).

Holders claim dividends on clanker.world from their profile or the token page. See [Holder Dividends](/general/holder-dividends). The burn share is burned in the launched token; see [Token Burns](/general/token-burns).

The Admin page also updates the image and metadata, verifies the token, and changes the reward recipients and admins. See [Managing a Token](/general/managing-a-token).

### Verification

A v5 token has no `verify()` function. To verify it, the current creator calls the Factory's `updateTokenExtraMetadata(token, "verified", "true")`. Only the exact value `true`, written after launch, shows the Verified badge.
