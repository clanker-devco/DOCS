---
title: "Unclaimed Rewards Balance"
---

If you've deployed a token with Clanker that has been traded at least once, you have earned rewards.

### Clanker v5

v5 rewards collect in the FeeEscrow, one balance per recipient and currency. Each balance includes the rewards of all v5 tokens that pay that wallet, because one claim pays all of it.

**On clanker.world**

1. Open the token's Admin page: `https://www.clanker.world/clanker/TOKEN_CONTRACT_ADDRESS_HERE/admin`.
2. The **Claim Rewards** card shows the unclaimed balance of the selected reward recipient, in the pair token (ETH or USDC).
3. A past recipient keeps its unclaimed balance. Connect that wallet and check the **Tax Fees** card on your [clanker.world profile](https://www.clanker.world/profile).

**With the API**

`GET /api/creator-earnings/{tokenAddress}` returns the unclaimed FeeEscrow balances of the token's current reward recipients. See [Creators](/api-reference/public/creators).

### v4 and earlier

You can check the balance of unclaimed rewards that you've accrued by looking up the balance of `Unclaimed Fees` on the initial LP of your token.

1. Go to your token's page on [https://www.clanker.world/clanker](https://www.clanker.world/clanker) and select `BaseScan`

   ![unclaimed rewards step 1](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-1.png)
2. Click on the contract address for the token's contract

   ![unclaimed rewards step 2](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-2.png)
3. Click on the contract creation transaction

   ![unclaimed rewards step 3](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-3.png)
4. Obtain the `Token ID` for the LP

   ![unclaimed rewards step 4](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-4.png)
5. Visit [`https://app.uniswap.org/positions/v3/base/[YOUR TOKEN ID HERE]`](https://app.uniswap.org/positions/v3/base/1119330) and replace the bracketed section with your `Token ID` from the previous step.&#x20;
6. 40% of the `Uncollected fees` is the current unclaimed rewards balance that token creators can claim. Please note that the dollar values of the rewards is an estimate and will fluctuate along with the prices of the underlying tokens in the pool, $CLANKER and $WETH in this case.\
   \
   Using $CLANKER as an example, the creator could claim:\
   \
   148.28 $CLANKER \* 40% = **\~59.312 $CLANKER** in creator rewards\
   3.73 $WETH \* 40% = **\~1.492 $WETH** in creator rewards

   ![unclaimed rewards step 6](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-6.png)
7. Please see [FAQ](/general/faq) for detail on claiming creator rewards.
