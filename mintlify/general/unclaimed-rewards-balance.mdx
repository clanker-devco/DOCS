---
title: "Unclaimed Rewards Balance"
---

Once a token you deployed with Clanker has been traded at least once, it has earned rewards. You can check what you can claim on clanker.world, through the API or, for older tokens, on chain.

## Clanker v5

### How it works

v5 rewards collect in the FeeEscrow, one balance per recipient and currency (ETH or USDC). A recipient's balance includes the rewards of all v5 tokens that pay that wallet, and one claim pays all of it, so claiming on one token also clears the balance shown on the recipient's other v5 tokens.

### Example

A wallet is the reward recipient of two ETH-paired v5 tokens. Token A has earned 0.3 ETH and token B 0.2 ETH since the last claim.

| Step                          | Amount  | Calculation            |
| ----------------------------- | ------- | ---------------------- |
| Balance shown on either token | 0.5 ETH | 0.3 + 0.2              |
| Paid by one claim             | 0.5 ETH | Whole balance          |
| Balance after the claim       | 0 ETH   | Cleared on both tokens |

### Check on clanker.world

1. Open the token's Admin page: `https://www.clanker.world/clanker/TOKEN_CONTRACT_ADDRESS_HERE/admin`.
2. In the **Claim Rewards** card, select **Check Claimable Fees** to see the unclaimed balance of the selected reward recipient, in the pair token.

A past recipient keeps its unclaimed balance. Connect that wallet and check the **Tax Fees** card on your [clanker.world profile](https://www.clanker.world/profile).

### With the API

`GET /api/creator-earnings/{tokenAddress}` returns the unclaimed FeeEscrow balances of the token's current reward recipients. See [Creators](/api-reference/public/creators).

## Clanker v4

Open the token's Admin page and select **Check Claimable Fees** in the **Claim Rewards** card. It shows the selected reward recipient's unclaimed rewards in the token and in the pair token.

## Clanker v3.1 and earlier

The initial LP of these tokens is a Uniswap v3 position, so you can check that position's **Uncollected fees** on chain:

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the token on BaseScan">


Go to your token's page on [clanker.world](https://www.clanker.world/) and select **BaseScan**.

![unclaimed rewards step 1](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-1.png)

</Step>


<Step title="Open the token contract">


Click the contract address of the token.

![unclaimed rewards step 2](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-2.png)

</Step>


<Step title="Open the creation transaction">


Click the contract creation transaction.

![unclaimed rewards step 3](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-3.png)

</Step>


<Step title="Find the LP token ID">


Note the **Token ID** of the LP position.

![unclaimed rewards step 4](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-4.png)

</Step>


<Step title="Open the position on Uniswap">


Visit `https://app.uniswap.org/positions/v3/base/YOUR_TOKEN_ID`, with your **Token ID** in place of `YOUR_TOKEN_ID` (for example, [this position](https://app.uniswap.org/positions/v3/base/1119330)).

</Step>


<Step title="Work out the creator's part">


The creator's part of the **Uncollected fees** depends on the version. On v3.1, Clanker takes 20% and the creator and interface share the other 80% (see [LpLockerv2](/references/core-contracts/v3.1/lplockerv2)). For the older CLANKER token in the screenshot, the creator's part is 40%. Dollar values are estimates and move with the prices of the two tokens in the pool.

![unclaimed rewards step 6](/assets/unclaimed-rewards-step-6.png)

</Step>


</Steps>

For the CLANKER position in the screenshot:

| Token    | Uncollected fees | Creator's part (40%) |
| -------- | ---------------- | -------------------- |
| CLANKER  | 148.28           | ~59.312              |
| WETH     | 3.73             | ~1.492               |
